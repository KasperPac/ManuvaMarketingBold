One quote, attributed. Lead with a metric when there is one — a number the customer can point at beats an adjective.

```jsx
<Testimonial metric="9" unit="minutes" quote="We had our first BOM running before lunch."
  name="Dana Reyes" role="Operations Manager" company="Ridgeline Manufacturing" field="cobalt"/>
```

Omit `field` for the calm card treatment. Never invent a quote.
