Opens a marketing section. The app equivalent is `PageHeader` — do not use this inside the product.

```jsx
<SectionHeader eyebrow="One system, six domains" title="Everything the floor touches."
  lede="Inventory, BOMs, work orders and stock control — connected and live." />
```

`tone="onField"` when it sits on a colour field, so it inherits the field's ink instead of `--ink-strong`.
