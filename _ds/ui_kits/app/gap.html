WhatShippedSystemWhy
SidebarNavy, centred logoNavy, left-aligned logoKept. The navy was already right — it comes from the logo. The lockup moves left so it sits on the same optical axis as the nav labels.
PrimaryIndigo #4F46E5Cobalt #3A5EFFIndigo is the default tint of the SaaS shelf and goes muddy next to navy. Cobalt is pure blue and pushed brighter so it reads as signal against the substrate, not as more navy.
GreysBlue-slateWarm graphiteCool navy surfaces against warm mid-greys is the contrast the system is built on. Slate everywhere flattens it.
KPI row7 equal cards, one number eachSame cards, plus a StateBandSeven equal numbers means no number is the point. The band says the one thing to act on; the cards stay for reference.
TypeSystem sans throughoutArchivo display, Inter UINumbers are the product. A display face with a width axis lets a metric be 40px and a table cell 13px without them looking like different products.
Empty states“No inventory movements in the last 6 months.”EmptyState with a numeralAn empty chart is the most common state in a new tenant and currently the least designed one.
ImpersonationAmber bar, “Viewing as…”UnchangedAlready correct — full width, undismissable, warning-toned. Lifted into the shell as-is.
Status colourRed 57%, amber “Low stock”Same roles, AA valuesMeanings kept exactly. Only the amber darkens to #8A5200, which the shipped value fails at 11–13px.
