Naming

Four layers. Reference only ever points down.

1 · PaletteRaw. Theme-independent. Literals live here and nowhere else.
--<hue>-<step> --graphite-950 --cobalt-500 --navy-800 — a 0→1000 ramp per hue. Never referenced by a component directly.
2 · SemanticTheme-scoped. What a thing IS, not what colour it is.
--<role>-<variant> --bg-card --ink-muted --stroke-strong --brand-1 --danger-dim — redefined per theme under [data-theme]. This is the layer components read.
3 · ScopedRebound by an ancestor attribute, not by a theme.
--accent-<use> under [data-domain] --accent-loud --accent-text --accent-dim --accent-on. One component, six identities: <div data-domain="purchasing"> repoints all four without the component knowing which module it is in.
4 · SurfaceMarketing only. The app never reads these.
--field-<name> · --on-<name> · --tint-<name> A field is a background and always travels with its ink: --field-amber pairs with --on-amber. Never a field without its --on-.
Reference scheme — two real chains
--info→ --brand-1→ --cobalt-500→ #3A5EFF
--accent-textunder [data-domain="purchasing"] → --domain-purchasing-text→ #8A5A00
Three hops maximum, and every hop goes one layer down. A semantic token may point at a palette token; a palette token may never point at a semantic one, and nothing points sideways within its own layer.
Do background: var(--bg-card)
color: var(--accent-text)
padding: var(--space-4) var(--space-6) Read layer 2 or 3. Let the theme and the domain decide.
Don't background: var(--cobalt-500)
color: #3A5EFF
padding: 16px 24px Reaching past the semantic layer freezes a component to one theme — Midnight, Ocean and Ember all break silently.
Dimensional tokens follow the same shape --space-1…32 (4px step) · --fs-xs…2xl then --fs-display-1…8 · --radius-1…poster · --control-sm…xl · --dur-* / --ease-*. Numbered scales for anything with a natural order; named ends only where the order is arbitrary.
